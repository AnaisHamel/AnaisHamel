### 👩‍💻 About me  

👋 Hi, I’m Anaïs Hamel, a data analyst with a PhD in cognitive science and psychology.
My background combines rigorous research methods with hands-on data analysis, applied to complex behavioral and neuroimaging datasets.

During my doctorate, I worked on large, multimodal datasets (behavioral, EEG, fMRI) within a clinical research project focused on aging and mental health.
I translated scientific questions into structured analytical pipelines, interpretable results, and actionable insights, bridging theory, data, and decision-making.

My research explored the interactions between sleep, meditation, memory, lifestyle, and brain functioning, with a strong emphasis on data quality, statistical validity, and interpretability.

I am now focused on applying these skills to data analyst roles, while remaining open to research-oriented analytical environments.
